# Netflix-Tableau-Dashboard
![image alt](https://github.com/subasri-13/Netflix-Tableau-Dashboard/blob/e7ca8b598b47f2c86012fa0d8de2e33d9afcf8fd/netflixdashboard.png)
PROJECT EXPLANATION:
This Tableau project analyzes Netflix Movies and TV Shows data to understand content distribution, growth, and trends across different dimensions.
It helps users understand:
Content growth over years 

Movies vs TV Shows distribution 

Popular genres 

Country-wise content availability 

Audience ratings insights

Content Growth by Year:

Area chart shows total Movies and TV Shows by year

Strong growth after 2015

Movies show higher growth compared to TV Shows

Highlights Netflix’s rapid content expansion

Movies vs TV Shows Distribution:

Movies: 4,068 titles (≈ 70.7%)

TV Shows: 1,688 titles (≈ 29.3%)

Shows Netflix focuses more on movies than TV shows

Top 10 Genres:

Bar chart displays the most popular genres:

International Movies – highest count

Dramas

Comedies

International TV Shows
Documentaries

Content by Country:

World map shows total titles by country

United States contributes the most content

Other major contributors include India, UK, and Canada

Shows Netflix’s strong global presence

Ratings Distribution:

Bar chart shows content by rating category

TV-MA and TV-14 have the highest number of titles

Indicates Netflix targets teen and adult audiences more

Filters & Interactivity:

Title selector for detailed analysis

Type filter (Movie / TV Show)

Interactive charts for easy exploration

Visual indicators for better understanding

Business Value This dashboard helps:

Content teams analyze genre popularity

Strategy teams understand audience targeting

Decision-makers track content growth trends

Analysts practice storytelling with data

Helps identify Netflix’s most produced content categories
